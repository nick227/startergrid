A destination may require you to reconnect when a sign-in expires, a key is revoked, or account permissions change. Check the connection result rather than assuming every password change breaks every integration.

## What to do

1. Open that destination on Channels.
2. Sign in again, or ask support to enter the new file-transfer details.
3. Use the connection-check action if the destination provides one, and review its result.

Do not re-import inventory to fix a sign-in problem. After reconnecting, check the delivery method and Autopilot selection before expecting another update.
