The inventory age panel groups vehicles by time recorded in StarterGrid. Choose a group to review those vehicles.

A suggested price is a recommendation, not an automatic change or a guaranteed sale. Check the vehicle’s condition, costs and current asking price before applying it.

## Change a price deliberately

1. In Vehicles, open the available price suggestion and review its current price, proposed price, and reduction. Days in StarterGrid may differ from the time the vehicle has physically been on your lot.
2. Choose the displayed **Apply** price action only if it matches your dealership’s pricing decision. Choose **Keep Current Price** to leave the price unchanged.
3. Check the saved price in Vehicles and then the listing results on Channels.

Saving a price updates StarterGrid’s inventory. Publication depends on the destination’s supported delivery method and settings. It does not guarantee an immediate update or a price-drop badge on every website.
