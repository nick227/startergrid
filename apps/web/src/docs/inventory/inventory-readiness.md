A saved vehicle is not automatically listed. It can sit on Vehicles until it has a price, photos, and a connected destination.

## What the status means

| Status | Meaning | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready to send | Required details are present | Send it, or leave Autopilot on for selected destinations |
| Needs details | Some destinations may still reject it | Add the extra fields or photos they ask for |
| Cannot list | A required item is missing | Fix price, photos, VIN, or availability |

## Destinations first

Channels controls which sites can receive vehicles. Only connected destinations appear as options on a vehicle. Your StarterGrid storefront is the first one to turn on because you do not sign in to another company.

## Ready to send is not published

Turning a vehicle ready, or checking a destination on that vehicle, does not put it live. It only means StarterGrid is allowed to send it. A listing is sent after you send it or Autopilot sends it. Sent is not a verified-live confirmation.

## After you fix a vehicle

Save the row. Ready vehicles can go out on the next send. Sold, removed, or zero-price vehicles may be excluded from new publication. Check the removal result for existing listings; an inventory change does not prove an external site has removed them.
