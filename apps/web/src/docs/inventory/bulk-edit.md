Use bulk edit when selected vehicles should share the same value. Entering a price sets that exact price on every selected vehicle; it does not subtract a discount.

## What to do

1. Select the vehicles on Vehicles.
2. Fill in the fields you want to change. Available fields depend on the inventory category and can include price, mileage, condition, colors, and body style. Blank fields stay unchanged.
3. Choose **Apply**. Use **Clear** to clear the selection and entered values without applying them.

Photos and VIN are not bulk-edited. Open each vehicle or re-import those.

## What happens next

StarterGrid rechecks the lot and can send ready vehicles again. Vehicles that still cannot be listed stay held until their own problem is fixed. A price change does not skip a missing photo.
