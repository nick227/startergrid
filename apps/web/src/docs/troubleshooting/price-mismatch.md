A site shows the last price it accepted from StarterGrid. That can differ from your DMS or website if those were changed somewhere else.

## What to do

1. Confirm the asking price on Vehicles is the price you want sent.
2. If you only changed the DMS, update the price here or import again.
3. Open the destination on Channels and confirm the last update went through.
4. Wait for that site’s own refresh. Sent is not the same as already visible.

Doc fees and conditional rebates belong in the disclosure, not hidden inside the asking price. See [Advertising rules you still own](doc:law/advertising-disclaimers).
