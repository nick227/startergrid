Cannot list means at least one required check failed. The row can still show year, make, model, and photos.

## What to do

1. Open the vehicle and read the next action.
2. Check VIN, price, photo count, body style, and whether it is still available.
3. Remember a vehicle can be ready to send to one destination and held on another that needs extra fields.

VIN decode often leaves cab type, bed length, or body style blank. Fill those by hand if the destination asks for them.

See [Needs details vs cannot list](doc:inventory/warnings-vs-blocked).
