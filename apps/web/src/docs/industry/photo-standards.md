A vehicle cannot be listed until it has enough photos. Destinations can still reject a listing that passed that minimum.

## What to do

1. Add real photos of this VIN — not a title scan as the only image.
2. Put the front three-quarter shot first.
3. If a destination still rejects the listing, add the extra interior or background they ask for.

Units in recon often stay at Cannot list until the photographer finishes. Price and VIN can be correct the whole time.
