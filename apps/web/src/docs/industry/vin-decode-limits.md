A 17-character VIN can fill year, make, model, and sometimes trim. It does not finish every field destinations require.

## Often still blank

| Field | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Cab / bed | Decode style may not match the destination’s list |
| Packages | Options are often not in the VIN |
| Color | VIN does not store paint |
| Mileage | Never from the VIN |

A vehicle can decode cleanly and still be Cannot list for body style or photo count. Fill the gap on the vehicle or in the import file.

See [The vehicle looks complete but cannot be listed](doc:troubleshooting/blocked-but-complete).
