Internal staff use one dealership record to manage setup, people, inventory and activity. Each unfinished step explains what needs to happen next.

## Work through setup

1. Confirm the dealership’s details and assigned internal contact.
2. Invite the owner and check whether the invitation was emailed or needs to be shared.
3. Add representative inventory, including a price and photos. If there is no stock yet, mark **Waiting for stock**; the dealership stays in setup.
4. Complete destination setup and publish a vehicle to Auto Marketplace. A saved Autopilot preference does not publish a listing.
5. Confirm the owner has signed in and a marketplace inquiry has reached this dealership’s shared Leads inbox.

## Launch and handoff are separate

Launch records confirmed publication on Auto Marketplace. Handoff records that the owner can take over daily work and that the shared inbox has received an inquiry. A dealership can have a live listing while handoff is still waiting on the owner or inbox check.

The checks come from saved results, so staff do not tick them complete by hand. If a step is blocked, read its next action. Completing handoff does not turn on an external provider or prove that email, text-message or customer-management-system alerts were delivered.

Use **Open dealer view** to see the dealership’s daily screens while remaining signed in as yourself. The support banner shows which dealership you are helping and provides a return link.
