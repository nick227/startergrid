A **destination** is a marketplace or advertising site where you want to list vehicles. You manage destinations on **Channels**.

## Complete setup

1. Choose the destination you want to use.
2. Follow the next action shown. This may mean signing in, entering account details or contacting that company.
3. Check which delivery method is available. Some destinations need manual work or partner approval.
4. Review the vehicles you want to list. Missing prices, photos or other required details can prevent publication.

Connecting an account does not enable automatic updates by itself. Only destinations available for automatic updates can be selected in Autopilot.

## Understand the result

| Label or result | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Connected | Account setup is recorded. Check the delivery method and any remaining steps. |
| Sent or submitted | An update was sent. The destination may still need to process it. |
| Confirmed live | DealerGrid has a current confirmation that the listing is visible. |
| Needs attention | Read the reason and next action. The task may belong to you, support or the destination company. |

Auto Marketplace is DealerGrid’s own consumer website. External sites have their own accounts, rules and processing times. A setup guide or connection does not mean DealerGrid can automatically publish to that site.
