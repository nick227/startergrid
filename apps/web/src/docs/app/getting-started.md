Use the dealership name at the top of the page to confirm which business you are working on.

## Your daily screens

- **Home:** see what needs your attention and choose the next task.
- **Vehicles:** add stock, correct details and photos, and mark vehicles sold or removed.
- **Channels:** choose marketplace and advertising sites, complete their setup, and check listing results.
- **Leads:** follow up on buyer inquiries received by StarterGrid. Your team shares the status and notes.

Use **Settings** for dealership details, team access and notification preferences. **Reports** contains additional information about inventory and activity.

## Get help without leaving your work

Choose **Help** in the header. It opens relevant documentation in a separate tab, leaving your work in place. Search the documentation index for other questions, or follow related articles and tags. Close the documentation tab to return to your original screen.

## Know what has actually happened

A vehicle can be saved without being listed. A destination can be connected without a listing being live. Check the listing result on Channels before assuming a website has changed.

Read [Connect a destination](doc:app/channels), [Prepare a vehicle for listing](doc:inventory/inventory-readiness), or [Manage buyer inquiries](doc:app/lead-status).
