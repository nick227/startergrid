A dealership owner can manage teammates in **Settings → Team**. Internal staff use **People & access** in the dealership record. Available actions depend on your role.

## Invite someone

Check the person’s email and role before creating the invitation. Then read the delivery result:

- **Email sent:** the mail server accepted the message. If it does not arrive, ask the person to check their spam folder and confirm the address.
- **Link created:** email delivery is not configured. Copy the setup link and share it with the intended person.
- **Email failed:** the message was not sent successfully. Check the address and follow the retry or link-sharing option shown.

Resending creates a new setup link and invalidates the old one. Share the newest link.

## Accept an invitation

Open the setup link. New users choose a password. If you already have a StarterGrid account, enter that account’s password to accept access to the additional dealership.

If the link expired or was revoked, ask the person who invited you for a new one. An invitation does not let you view another person’s password.

## Temporary passwords and other sign-in problems

If staff gave you a temporary password, sign in with it and choose your own password before continuing. If your account is suspended, ask the dealership owner or internal support to review access. For a forgotten password, ask an authorized administrator to use the available recovery action; do not create a duplicate account.

If you can sign in but cannot find a dealership, ask the owner or internal support to check your dealership assignment.
