What you advertise is still your responsibility. StarterGrid can block a missing VIN or photo count. It does not interpret state fee rules or OEM co-op language.

## What to do

- Put the obtainable asking price in the price field. Doc fees and conditional rebates belong in the disclosure your state requires.
- Mark sold or removed the day the deal funds. Do not leave a funded unit available.
- Use photos of the actual vehicle. Stock art or last year’s body style is treated as misrepresentation on several sites.
- Hold a listing if compliance has not approved the copy.

A vehicle can be ready to send here and still fail a destination’s own review.
