Auto-Response attempts to send an acknowledgment after a new inquiry. It does not replace your team’s follow-up or guarantee delivery.

## What to do

1. Open **Settings → How you hear about new inquiries → Optional delivery methods**.
2. Turn on Auto-Response. It stays off until you do this.
3. Choose email, text, or both. Text also needs the buyer’s phone and texting enabled by support.
4. Keep the default message or write your own with `{name}`, `{vehicle}`, and `{dealer}`.

## What happens next

The inquiry is saved before delivery is attempted. Review recorded results and failures in **Leads → Delivery log**. Auto-Response only runs on the first inquiry — it does not answer follow-ups.

Keep texts short. Ask your compliance contact before you turn this on in your state.
