Auto-Response thanks the buyer right after they ask about a vehicle, so they are not waiting in silence.

## What to do

1. Open How you hear about new inquiries.
2. Turn on Auto-Response. It stays off until you do this.
3. Choose email, text, or both. Text also needs the buyer’s phone and texting enabled by support.
4. Keep the default message or write your own with `{name}`, `{vehicle}`, and `{dealer}`.

## What happens next

The inquiry is saved, then the thank-you goes out. Sent messages appear in Communications, including failures. Auto-Response only runs on the first inquiry — it does not answer follow-ups.

Keep texts short. Ask your compliance contact before you turn this on in your state.
