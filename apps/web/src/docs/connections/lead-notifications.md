When a buyer asks about a vehicle, StarterGrid can notify every method you turned on. One method failing does not stop the others.

## What to do

Open **Settings → How you hear about new inquiries → Optional delivery methods**. Add only what your team will use, then save. Internal staff can use the same section in the dealership profile.

| Method | What you add | Who sets it up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Primary contact email on the dealership profile | You |
| Discord | A webhook URL from your Discord channel | You |
| Telegram | A bot token and chat ID | You |
| Webhook | An HTTPS address that accepts a POST | You or your integrator |
| SMS | A mobile number | You, after support enables texting |

## What you receive

Email and integration alerts can include vehicle and buyer details; SMS contains a shorter summary. Check **Leads → Delivery log** for recorded delivery outcomes. A saved setting does not confirm delivery, and acceptance by a mail server does not confirm that someone read the message.
